## Step 1f — why the full-fold NLL reverses the stratum's ruling

| scored under | censored share | delta on rows with an observed ending | delta on censored rows |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `standard` labels | 0.483 | **−0.100373** | **+0.152242** |
| `10b` labels | 0.542 | **−0.098392** | **+0.155446** |

| | mean predicted median life |
| :--- | ---: |
| A0 `standard` | **20.680 laps** |
| A1 `10b` | **25.996 laps** (+26%) |

A censored row scores through `log S(t) = log P(T > t)`, monotonically improved by predicting a
longer life. The `10b` arm predicts 26% longer, so it is rewarded on the ~half of the fold that is
censored without having to be right about anything, and penalised 0.10 on the rows where an ending
was observed. The uncensored-row delta (−0.1004) ≈ the green-pit delta (−0.0984), as it must:
green-pit is 9,149 of 10,334 uncensored eval rows under `standard`, 88.5%.
