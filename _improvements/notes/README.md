# `notes/` — traced answers to one-off questions

Short, self-contained findings that came out of a direct question rather than a work item or a
research track. Each note answers one question, carries its evidence, and states plainly whether
the answer is Verified or Assumed per [`../foundations/epistemics.md`](../foundations/epistemics.md).

**Not a tracker, and not a commitment.** Nothing here has a stage. A note that turns into work
gets promoted into [`../status/build-log.json`](../status/build-log.json) like anything else.
