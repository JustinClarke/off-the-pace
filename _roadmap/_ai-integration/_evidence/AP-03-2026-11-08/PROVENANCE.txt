2. Season Structural Pace interval  (constructor-structural-pace/queries.ts season branch, transform.ts seasonMeanSe)
  Derivation. Season point = plain mean of the k per-race estimates, m = (1/k) Σ p_i.
    Var(m) = (1/k²) Σ_i Σ_j Cov(p_i, p_j). If the per-race estimation errors are independent
    (each race is fitted on its own laps: int_constructor_structural_pace.sql groups by race, the
    field-pace curve it subtracts is per race), Cov = 0 for i≠j, so Var(m) = Σ se_i² / k² and
    SE(m) = sqrt(Σ se_i²) / k.  CI = m ± 1.96·SE(m)  (per-race CIs are exactly ±1.96·SE:
    max |ci_high - p - 1.96·se| = 2.2e-16 race, 3.6e-15 quali).
    Estimand: the average over THIS season's races of each race's structural pace. Circuit-to-circuit
    variation in the true pace is not sampling noise for that estimand. If independence fails, or the
    per-race SEs are understated (they are SD/sqrt(n) of lap deltas for a MEDIAN, ignoring lap-to-lap
    autocorrelation; the asymptotic SE of a median under normality is already 1.25x that), the
    interval is too narrow -> stated as a LOWER BOUND in methodology.tsx.
  Choice: option (a) (plain mean, sqrt(Σse²)/k). Reasons:
    - the season point fans see does not move (only the interval changes);
    - option (b) (precision-weighted) moves points (race: median |shift| 0.046 s, max 0.170 s; quali
      median 0.073 s, max 0.852 s) and is UNDEFINED in quali: 10 qualifying rows have se = 0, giving
      infinite weight (e.g. McLaren 2025 quali: weighted mean NaN, SE 0);
    - (b) is the fixed-effect meta-analysis estimator, whose SE is the smaller of the two and is
      valid only if every race estimates the same true value, which the data reject (below).
    Not mixed: point and SE are both the plain mean's.
  NULL handling: 42 of 1,644 race rows have NULL pace (CO-04), and their SE is NULL with them
    (count(pace) = count(se) = 1,602). SUM(se²) and COUNT(pace) skip the same rows; k = COUNT(pace).
    races_n is now that k (was COUNT(*)): changes the "Races" column in 22 of 81 race-mode

3. Party Mode delta SE  (party-mode/queries.ts, transform.ts, page.tsx, methodology.tsx)
  Race grain: delta_se = sqrt(se_q² + se_r²) (independence of the two sessions' estimation errors
    assumed; stated). Season grain: se_r = sqrt(Σse_r²)/k_r and se_q = sqrt(Σse_q²)/k_q over the
    joined races (step-2 rule), then combined the same way. CI = delta ± 1.96·delta_se.
  SQL returns race_se_s / quali_se_s; transform.ts deltaSe()/deltaInterval() add delta_se_s,
    delta_ci_low_s, delta_ci_high_s, delta_ci_covers_zero to each entry. CSV gains delta_se_s,
    delta_ci_low_s, delta_ci_high_s (3 dp). Table gains a "Delta 95% CI" column. Point deltas and
    the party/balanced/race labels are UNCHANGED. The covers-zero flag is computed on the entry
    only; it is not shown and not in the CSV, and no third label ships (owner decision).
  Counts (scratch warehouse):
    race grain: 1,644 joined rows, 1,602 with a delta (42 have NULL race pace, CO-04).
      median delta SE 0.168 s (> the 0.15 s band). 378 balanced, 1,224 labelled party/race.
      855 have a 95% CI covering 0; 478 of those are LABELLED party (283) or race (195).
      (spec estimated "about 477"; exact is 478.)
    season grain: 81 cells; median delta SE 0.060 s. 24 balanced, 57 labelled.
      23 CIs cover 0; 6 of those are LABELLED party/race.
