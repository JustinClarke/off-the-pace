Deviations / findings for the owner
  1. ONE_SKILL_MEASURE "single-card form" (any skill card must name its measure) is NOT implemented. The rule
     format has one required_wording per rule, so a second trigger with min 1 would force single-card answers to
     say "different measures". Options: (a) an 8th rule ONE_SKILL_MEASURE_NAMED with trigger
     skill_measure matches "." (breaks the spec's 24 count; AX-21 makes it 30), or (b) leave it to AX-21's
     glossary rules. Recommended (b).
  2. skill_measure is not yet a field in card.schema.json policy_fields (CardPolicyFields has trust_label,
     car_term_source, calibration_flag, is_in_envelope, beats_baseline_significant). toCheckerCard spreads
     policy_fields to top level, so the rule reads field "skill_measure". AX-07 / AX-15a must add it to the
     schema when the card kinds set it.
  3. THIN_SAMPLE (existing, untouched) fires on every held or unavailable card with n_unit "stints", because
     AX-01 requires n.count = 0 on those cards and THIN_SAMPLE tests n < 8. Found when an unavailable pit-loss
     example failed the whole policy; the example was moved to a laps-unit card. Held pit cards (AX-04's
     pit_strategy.optimal_lap) will therefore demand thin-sample wording. Suggest adding {status ne held,
     label ne unavailable} to THIN_SAMPLE's second trigger (owner; it is one of the 17 frozen rules).
  4. CALIBRATION_FLAGGED_CIRCUIT has no producer: calibration_flag is absent from dim_circuits and
     export_app_data.py (only in policy.json, page summaries and transform/seeds/circuit_reference.csv). It is a
     transform + export change, not done here; flag as its own item. (The spec asked for a not_fired example
     recording the gap; not added, because it would edit one of the 17 frozen rules. The gap is recorded here.)
  5. DD-WC2 (exact disclaimer for modelled traffic-cost stints) is deferred to AX-16, as the spec says.
  6. Held reasons the rule accepts: wake calibration, ML/model redo, re-measure, reproduction rate, dirty-air
     calibration. AX-04's transitional reason "CLASS_NOT_LIVE" is not among them; once AX-07 makes T live only
     DA/TRAFFIC/ML/pit cards stay held, and those map onto the list.
  7. Fragile citations: BUILD-ORDER.md:23 (rules part above the generated block) and design-ai-access.md:91/143
     are cited by line; an insertion above them fails sources.node.test.ts until the line is bumped.
  No contradiction found by the new rules; contradictions list unchanged.
