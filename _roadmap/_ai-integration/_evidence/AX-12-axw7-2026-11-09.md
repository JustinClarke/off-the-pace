## Findings

2. Reasoning: tagged "Reasoning"; effort levels low, medium (default), high. The page does not say which request parameter sets effort (the Worker sends `REASONING = {effort: "low"}` in config.ts; this shape is not confirmed by the page).

4. Reasoning tokens vs the cap: the page does NOT say whether reasoning tokens count toward `max_tokens`; `max_completion_tokens` is not mentioned. Still UNVERIFIED from docs.
5. The function-calling guide page does not list gpt-oss-20b (it names only hermes-2-pro as an example, and points to the catalogue property); it says nothing on reasoning or limits.
