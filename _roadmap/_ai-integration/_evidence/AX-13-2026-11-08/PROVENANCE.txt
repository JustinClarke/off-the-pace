OWNER DECISIONS / FINDINGS
  1. Bar as written: no change to the spec's thresholds. "No run over 18,000 tokens" is defined on budget.total (prompt + completion
     summed over the run's rounds, what neurons are billed on), not on the per-round peak context (bounded at 13,100 by the slots).
     Say if the peak was meant.
  2. Not gating, by the spec: the honesty checker pass rate, expected_value, rules_fired. Recommend adding "100% honesty checker pass"
     to the bar for AX-20; it is reported in every summary.
  3. Capacity vs the eval: 5 repeats x 79 questions cannot run inside one day's 10,000 neurons; run over days with --resume, or approve
     a paid day. VISITOR_DAILY_LIMIT (3) must be raised for the eval run (local --var only).
  4. The model projection carries no display and no policy_fields: the model cannot see rating_trust_label (thin / suppressed),
     rank_separated_from_next, coverage_reason or a result's grid slot, though system.md tells it to act on the first two. Suppressed
     and unrated come through `why`; thin only through n. Option: add trust/rank fields to the projection (cards/project.ts, ~40 chars
     per card).
  5. No tool path for "how many races did X win": actual_finish_position is a measure in qv_driver_race (not filterable, not a
     group_by), and race.result cards are one per race (> the 10-card cap). Option: make it a dimension, or a season wins card.
  6. query_data allows at most 2 seasons per request (too_many_seasons) even on views whose catalogue max_seasons is null; a
     "per season" chart over 2018-2025 needs 4 calls (the tool cap) or a season card.
  7. worker/src/generated/tools.ts stays generated from test/fixtures (old 10-kind index): the real run must regenerate it from the
     data (step 1). AX-10 finding 4 still open.
  8. system.md grew 931 -> 990 tokens (chars/4; 1,132 by estimateTokens, slot 1,200) for the teammate_race sentences.
  9. h-ml-band (ML prediction band): no card kind exists yet (pending AX-17), so get_fact_cards rejects the kind; the right answer
     comes from system.md alone. Counted under "no tool could answer" until AX-17.
