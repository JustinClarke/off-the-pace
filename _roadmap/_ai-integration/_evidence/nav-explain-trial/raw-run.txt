Q [explain] What does the cliff survival profile show?
  answer: The cliff‑survival profile displays a Kaplan‑Meier survival curve that represents the probability a tyre stint remains **pre‑degradation‑cliff** on each lap. The chart overlays several elements:

* **Survival curve S(t)** – the stepwise probability that a stint of the selected compound is still alive at lap t, updated only when a cliff event occurs.  
* **Model onset line** – the median cliff lap derived from the fitted survival model stored in the **dim_compounds_season** dimension (card [tyre‑cliff‑survival]).  
* **Real‑stint scatter** – red dots for stints that hit a cliff before pitting, grey squares for stints that pitted before a cliff.  

Censored observations (pits before a cliff) reduce the at‑risk pool but do not count as an event. Rain laps and flagged anomalies are excluded from both the curve and the scatter. The point where the survival probability declines to half its starting value marks the median expected cliff lap for that compound and circuit.  

*(All data come from the tyre‑cliff‑survival card.)*

Q [explain] What is the blind test scoreboard page for?
  answer: The blind‑test scoreboard page lets you see how the **degradation model** matches what actually happened on each lap in a season you pick.  

It displays three panels:

1. **Predicted‑vs‑actual scatter** – each dot is one lap; the horizontal axis shows the real cumulative degradation jump over the following five laps, and the vertical axis shows the model’s 50 % prediction.  
2. **Interval coverage rug** – a bar chart that indicates how often the real jump falls inside the model’s confidence band.  
3. **Cliff‑class confusion matrix** – a table showing the proportion of laps for which the model’s predicted “laps until the next major degradation” matches the real outcome.  

Because the models are trained on every season that has existed, the numbers you see are an in‑sample fit; out‑of‑sample validation occurs only when a held‑out season is added. The scoreboard is therefore a way to check that the models track reality, not a simulation of future races.

Q [explain] What is the blind test scoreboard page for?
  answer: The blind‑test scoreboard page shows how well the shipped degradation models track what actually happened. It compares the model’s lap‑by‑lap predictions with the real cumulative degradation for the season you select, using three visual panels:

1. **Predicted‑vs‑actual scatter** – each point represents a lap; points lying on the 45° line indicate perfect predictions.  
2. **Interval coverage rug** – the fraction of laps whose real jump falls inside the model’s 80 % confidence band.  
3. **Cliff‑class confusion matrix** – how the predicted “cliff” categories compare to the observed categories, with the diagonal indicating correct predictions.

All numbers displayed come from the model artefacts (e.g., `mart_degradation_predictions.parquet` joined with `fct_cliff_prediction_features` and `fct_stint_features`). No hand‑typed figures are used. The page is intended to illustrate in‑sample fit; true out‑of‑sample validation will only appear once 2026 data are ingested.
