## The problem (re-verified 2026-11-07; numbers from the scratch warehouse)

1. **Pit confidence is dropped.** `pit-strategy/queries.ts:126` selects `optimal_pit_lap_confidence` (typed at `:22`). `transform.ts:27-40` never copies it onto `GanttStint` (which has no such field, `Gantt.tsx:11-27`), and `toCsvRows` (`:72-87`) omits it.
   - The column is never NULL: 8,211 rows, range 0.0-0.986.
   - `schema.yml:2526-2531` defines it: the share of candidate laps more than 1 s worse than the optimum. 0.0 means no curve exists, near 0 means a flat curve.
2. **The season Structural Pace interval is the average of per-race intervals.** `constructor-structural-pace/queries.ts:62-65` uses `AVG(pace)`, `AVG(se)`, `AVG(ci_low)` and `AVG(ci_high)`. The point estimate is a plain mean of races, but its SE is not the mean's SE.
   - Over 81 season × team cells, the median averaged SE is **0.112 s**. The SE of the plain mean, if races are independent (`sqrt(Σse²)/k`), is **0.026 s**: 4.3× smaller.
   - So today's season interval is too *wide* if races are independent. It is somewhere between the two if they are correlated.
   - Per-race CIs are exactly ±1.96 × SE (checked on all 1,644 rows).
3. **The Party Mode delta has no SE.**
   - `party-mode/queries.ts:40` (race) and `:58` (season) compute `quali − race`. Both inputs carry `constructor_structural_pace_se_s`.
   - `transform.ts:15-17` labels a team "party" or "race" when |delta| > 0.15 s.
   - At race grain, the median delta SE `sqrt(se_q² + se_r²)` is **0.168 s**, wider than the 0.15 s band.
   - Of 1,644 race deltas, 378 fall inside the band, but **855 have a 95% CI that covers 0**. About 477 race-level labels therefore rest on a delta indistinguishable from zero.
   - The audit also notes the two inputs use different lap populations, and Party Mode is derived from Structural Pace, so it is not independent evidence.

## Method

1. **Pit confidence.** Add `optimalPitLapConfidence: number | null` to `GanttStint`. Map it in `transform.ts`, add `optimal_pit_lap_confidence` to the CSV (3 dp), and show it in the stint tooltip with the schema's meaning. Show 0.0 as "no cost curve", not "0% confident".
2. **Season interval.** Choose one estimator, say why, and write it in the feature's `methodology.tsx`:
   - (a) keep the plain mean and use `SE = sqrt(Σse²)/k` (this assumes races are independent; say so);
   - (b) switch to the precision-weighted mean `Σ(p/se²)/Σ(1/se²)` with `SE = 1/sqrt(Σ1/se²)`. This moves the point estimate too.

   Derive the CI as ±1.96 × SE. Do not mix a plain-mean point with a weighted-mean SE (the audit's fix #3 pairs them; that is inconsistent). If independence is doubtful, say the interval is a lower bound.
3. **Party Mode SE.**
   - Race grain: `delta_se = sqrt(se_q² + se_r²)` (assumes independence; state it).
   - Season grain: apply the step-2 rule to each series, then combine.

   Add `delta_se_s` and a CI to the row, the table and the CSV. Propose, but do not ship without the owner, a third label ("not distinguishable") for deltas whose CI covers 0.
4. Race-grain Structural Pace values and CIs must not change.
