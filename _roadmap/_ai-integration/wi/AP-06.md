# AP-06 - race-lost and lap-waterfall "pace delta" is not the identity it claims

**Group:** 2 app fixes · **Depends on:** AP-01 · **Model/cost:** sonnet-5, 0.5d · **Blocker:** none.

**Sources:** `app/src/features/race-lost/queries.ts` (lines 62-63), `app/src/features/lap-waterfall/queries.ts` (lines 64-65), AP-01 evidence.

## Problem
Both queries add `total_explained_s` and the driver-skill residual over DIFFERENT sets of laps, so the pace delta they display is not the sum of the terms it claims to close to. AP-01 removed the COALESCE-to-0 but kept per-component averages, so the mismatch can remain.

## Method
1. Define one lap set per query (laps where every term of the identity is non-NULL) and compute every term and the closure check over it; terms that cover more laps may be shown separately with their own n.
2. The closure badge compares the sum of terms to the pace delta over that same set; "n/a" only if the set is empty.
3. Report, for 5 races of your choice, how much the displayed pace delta changes (before vs after) in your PROVENANCE.

## Acceptance
For every race in a fixture the sum of displayed terms equals the displayed pace delta to display precision (test). A race with a NULL-only component keeps AP-01's "unavailable" bar.
