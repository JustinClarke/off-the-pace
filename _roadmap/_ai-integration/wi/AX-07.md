## Findings that change the method

1. **Wide marts defeat model-level lineage.** `fct_cliff_prediction_features` carries both push-load columns (driver-workload) and `dirty_air_*` columns. `fct_lap_residuals` and `fct_stint_features` mix observed and dirty-air columns. Decide per column the card actually uses.
2. **The literal model-level rule is wrong in both directions** (plan risk 1). A `ref()` grep puts `int_lap_air_state` and/or `int_event_corrections` upstream of `int_driver_race_skill_loro`, `int_field_pace_curve`, `int_track_evolution`, `int_pit_strategy_value` and `mart_corner_skill_driver`. Read literally, v1 would be left with race-control, results and data quality. HD13 resolves it: price-downstream means T\*; filter-only means T plus a caveat.
3. **Most T sources have no interval.** Race-control counts, field pace, workload, recovery, anomaly counts and results have `interval: none`. Under AX-01 only `measured` and `computed` cards may lack an interval. To show "a thin and a clean race differ in `n` and interval", use `race.teammate_gap` (`ok` versus `thin`/`suppressed`) or `corner-phase-skill` (`se_1`, n = cells).
4. **Hazard rates are NULL for 2018.** `int_sc_hazard_history` is season-lagged (`export_app_data.py:87-90` comment). Those cards are `unavailable` with a reason, never 0.
5. **Label and model versions need a source.** `data_version` is `manifest.version`. `label_version` comes from TX-05's `mart_build_provenance`. If it is not exported, stop and report; do not hard-code `v16`.
6. `pit-strategy` drops `optimal_pit_lap_confidence` in its transform (AP-03 fixes it). The pit-verdict cards stay `held`, with `held_reason` citing `PIT_CALL_REPRODUCTION_RATE` (D9) and watch AXW8.

## Method

1. **AX-07a, lineage first (day 1, 0.5d; the plan's spike, run inside this item).** On a scratch copy named `dev.duckdb` in your own folder (threads 1; never the live warehouse), run `../.venv/bin/dbt compile` and `dbt ls --select +<model>` from `transform/`.
   - **T\*:** `int_dirty_air_tax_component` or `int_driver_isolation_lap_panel` is upstream of the column used, or `theta_air` is used as a value. Match the compiled SQL, never comments.
   - **T with caveat `AIR_STATE_FILTER`:** the source reads `int_lap_air_state` only in a `WHERE`, `JOIN` or `QUALIFY` that chooses laps.
   - Then apply finding 1 per column.
   - Save `_roadmap/_ai-integration/_evidence/ax-07-lineage/lineage.md` and `lineage.json`: one row per source model and per used column, with its class, the rule that fired and the evidence line. AX-15a and AX-21 read this file. Report the T/T\* split before step 2.
2. **Write the cards.** A `cards.ts` for each T feature (AX-04's pattern), plus `sqlKind`s. The minimum v1 set, which AX-21's recipes use, is `race.result`, `race.teammate_gap`, `race.coverage`, `race.cautions`, `driver_race.pit_stops`, `data_quality.race`, `race_control.pooled_rate`, `tyre_cliff_survival.profile`, `corner_phase.season`, and `season.teammate_gap` if T. Each kind sets:
   - `class:'T'`, `label`, and `intervalKind` (from the model SQL, never guessed);
   - `nUnit`;
   - `caveatRules`: `THIN_SAMPLE` as the policy states it today (`n_unit = stints`, `n < 8`; any other threshold goes to the policy first); `IN_SAMPLE` for 2018 cliff cards (CD2); `AIR_STATE_FILTER` where step 1 found it;
   - `policy_fields` (`rating_trust_label`, `rank_separated_from_next`, `skill_measure`, `shared_input_group`, for AX-09 and AX-21);
   - `pageParams` (route and params, from AX-03) and `onScreen(params)` (at most 6 card ids).
3. **Held kinds:** pit verdict and opportunity cost (`status:'held'`, no value).
4. **Refusals.** A source row with no `n`, a label with no interval where one is required, or a column found T\* in step 1 is refused with a coded reason. Write the refusals to `_evidence/ax-07-lineage/ungrounded.json` (kind, count, reason). Report cards by kind and refusals by reason.
5. Register `classesLive = ['T']` only after the acceptance below passes.

## Acceptance

- Every card has every schema field, or it is refused with a reason (`validateCard` on all output; the counts match the refusals).
- A thin and a clean case differ visibly in `n` (every kind) and in interval (`race.teammate_gap` or corner-phase-skill).
- `interval.kind` equals the registry value on every card, and a test that relabels one expects a failure.
- The cards match the page for 20 (driver, race) pairs across at least 3 seasons on the driver-by-race kinds, and on 3 seasons of their own grain for the other kinds. The oracle is an independent SQL query over the same parquet at display precision. Pairs are chosen by a fixed seed and written into the test.
- No card has a `class` other than `T`, none reads `flag_chance_lookup.json` or a DA-03 artefact, and none carries a dirty-air figure. The lineage table is saved, and the provisional classes in design 2.4 are reconciled against it, with the differences listed (closes AXW11 with AX-15a).
