## Decided

- **Pass bar (from AI-07, do not change):** 100% of numbers grounded; 100% of refusal cases correct; the right tool used in 95% of runs or more; no run over 18,000 tokens; the human read passes.
- 5 repeats per question (spikes were single runs). Expected values are read from the published card table at grade time, never typed into the question file, so the set survives re-exports.
- Held classes (T\*, held in v1 because HD2 was ruled C, through AX-15a's stubs; traffic-cost; ML) must be **refused or hedged with the held reason**, never answered with a number (rule 4, rule 9, AD5).
- Capacity is measured here: the AX-12 usage readout turns neurons per answer into answers per day on the 10,000-neuron allowance (HD6's 12-40 a day is arithmetic, not a measurement).

## Method

1. **Question file** `app/src/assistant/eval/questions.json`, each: `id`, `text` (fan language), `type` (an AX-21 playbook type), `class` (T, T*, WC, ML, X, nav, whatif), `family`, `season(s)`, `expected_tools`, `expected_cards` (kind + scope, resolved at grade time), `expect` (`answer` | `refuse` | `hedge`), `held_reason` (when refusing), `rules_should_fire`. Minimum counts: every one of the 7 tools in at least 5 questions; at least 15 needing `query_data`; at least 8 refuse/hedge; at least 4 families and 3 seasons.
2. **Groups.** (a) T cards: one or more per live kind. (b) AX-21's v1 recipes, at least two questions per live type: `teammate_race`, `why_slow` (context cards, with race-lost shown held and its reason), `season_standing`, `stint_describe`, `safety_car_rate` (pooled rate only), `tyre_life`, `corner_phase`, `observed_count`. The audit's "why was D slow" via race-lost and "teammate" via era rating or ghost standings are tested here only as far as v1 classes allow. (c) Refuse/hedge: chains the audit forbids (per-circuit SC probability; ranking drivers across different "skill" measures, answers must name one definition; stacking affinity with era ratings; "would have won" from counterfactuals; blind test as out-of-sample; a grey lap-map cell as free air; sector attribution; 2026 races; cross-era pure pace; the chance line). (d) One group per held class: ML prediction bands, traffic cost, the AX-15a dirty-air-dependent stubs (race-lost breakdown, ghost car, constructor pace, and any rating AX-07a classed T\*), pit-call verdict (describe-only with the 33.9% wording). (e) Navigation, explain-page, what-if (link only), list_values alias.
3. **Grader** `eval/grade.ts`, per answer: every number matches a cited card or `result_id` (reuse `findUngroundedNumbers`, do not fork it); `checkAnswer` required wording present and no forbidden claim; refusal questions refused with the right held reason; the first expected tool was called; tokens under 18,000 (from AX-10's budget log). Output per check, per family, per class.
4. **Human read** of 10 answers (script prints them): the term is defined or linked the first time it appears; the answer says what the number does not show when a wrong reading is likely; tone is plain. Record pass/fail per answer with a note.
5. **Record the bar first.** Write the bar and the question-file hash to `_roadmap/_ai-integration/_evidence/ax-13/bar.json` and have the orchestrator append it to `status/build-log.json` history **before the first run**. Do not edit the log yourself unless the orchestrator says so; no run counts before the bar is recorded.
6. **Baseline run** against the real model (local endpoint, then the AX-12 preview Worker if deployed): 5 repeats. Save `baseline.json` (every run) and `baseline-summary.md` (score per check/family/class, every failure with cause, share of questions no tool could answer, mean tokens, neurons and cost per answer, answers per day at 10,000 neurons, final `MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS` recommendation, and whether reasoning tokens hit the cap).

## Acceptance

- Bar recorded before the first run (file hash matches the question file used).
- The grader fails a planted wrong answer for every question type: wrong card number, number with no source, missing required wording, a number given for a held class, answered when it should refuse, wrong reason given, wrong tool, over-budget run. One planted answer per type, in `grade.test.ts`.
- Counts in method step 1 are met (a test counts them).
- A baseline exists with tokens, neurons and cost per answer; failures are listed, not hidden.

## Tests to add

## Definition of done

Question set, grader and bar are recorded before the first run; one baseline run is saved under `_evidence/ax-13/`; tests pass. A failing baseline is a valid result: report it, do not tune the bar. Do not commit.
