## What is held, why, and what lifts it

**Held (WC class): every traffic-cost card.** That means the kinds for dirty air, held up, racecraft and tow/DRS, per stint and per driver-race total, and the on-screen cards for the `dirty-air-cost` and `dirty-air-lap-map` pages. They are published as `status:"held"`, `value:null` with `held_reason` = "traffic cost is being re-measured; no number is available yet". Reason: the only dirty-air number in the marts is the unvalidated flat 0.331 s/lap x share (label v16), and the archived DA-03 numbers are barred (rule 9). The assistant refuses traffic-cost questions with that reason (HD5-A).

**Lift:** all of (1) `XW` resolved with WC-05 and WC-06 landed; (2) the owner has approved the label and disclaimer on screen; (3) the WC marts are exported and the manifest re-exported (HD12 process); (4) HD2 re-ruled (A or B) and AX-15b landed. Then flip `HELD_CLASSES` to drop `WC`. Until then this item may only be built against fixtures.

## Decided (WC-06, owner 2026-10-27)

- Three labels on every number: **measured** (number with interval), **modelled** (wider interval, marked "modelled", with the disclaimer), **unavailable** ("—" and its reason, never 0).
- Disclaimer on every modelled value, exact text: "Normalised from other compounds, based on stints from other drivers or the general performance of the tyres after removing other factors."
- **Totals show the modelled share**, for example "3.4 s (28% modelled)". Unavailable laps are left out of totals and counted.
- Stint coverage the cards must carry: 16.7% of stints (1,177) have no clean lap; about 86% of those are modelled from another stint of the same driver or the teammate; about 2% of all stints (162) are unavailable. These are WC-06's figures; re-read them from the WC marts, do not hard-code.
- No DA-03 number anywhere; no 0.331 x share price presented as traffic cost.

## Method (once the lift conditions hold)

1. Read WC-05/WC-06 as landed; list the actual mart and column names and the label column. If they differ from WC-06's text, the marts win; record the differences.
2. Export the WC marts (`scripts/export_app_data.py`); add `cards.ts` for `dirty-air-cost` and `dirty-air-lap-map`: `traffic_cost.stint` (per stint, per component, label per stint because a stint can switch label) and `traffic_cost.race_total` (per driver-race, with `modelled_share` and `unavailable_count`). Use the page's own query and `transform()` run headless (P2).
3. Card mapping: label from the WC label column; `modelled` requires an interval or the card is refused; `unavailable` has `value:null` and `unavailable_reason`; `n` = laps (or stints); `interval.kind` copied from the registry. Add policy rule hooks so the AX-09 modelled-disclaimer rule fires on any modelled traffic card and on any total with a modelled share above 0.
4. Registry: `class:'WC'`. Add a deny-list test for source columns `dirty_air_tax_s`, `cumulative_dirty_air_tax_s` and any column from the archived method.
5. `onScreen(params)` for both pages returns the stint cards for the selected driver and race (at most 6).

## Acceptance

- Every modelled card and every total with a modelled share carries the exact disclaimer (policy rule passes on a fixture, fails when the text is altered).
- A total shows its modelled share and the count of unavailable laps; an all-unavailable stint is `unavailable`, not 0, and does not enter the total.
- A driver whose stint switches label shows the label per stint, not one label for the race.
- No DA-03 number or 0.331-based price in any card (test); the chance line is not referenced.
- While `XW` is open: 100% of WC cards are `held` with no value.
- Parity with the page for 20 (driver, race) pairs across at least 3 seasons.
