## Method

1. **Snapshot still holds.** Run `python3 scripts/assistant_freeze.py --verify`. If anything moved, stop and reopen AX-19 (watch W2). Confirm the held classes in `freeze.json` match what the eval expects to be refused.
2. **Build.** Build the app with `VITE_ASSISTANT_ENABLED=true` and `VITE_DATA_BASE` pointing at the frozen data; Worker on the owner's preview deploy with the real default cap (at most 10,000 neurons) and per-visitor limit.
3. **Run AX-13's set**, 5 repeats per question, through the **real drawer** (Playwright drives the flag-on build, real Worker, real model) on the frozen snapshot. Do not use the CLI for the scored run.
4. **Score against the bar AX-13 recorded before its first run.** Do not change the bar.
5. **Record cost.** From `/admin/usage` before and after (needs the owner's admin token; the agent never stores it): questions, rounds, neurons; compute neurons per answer, answers per day on 10,000 neurons, and compare with HD6's 12-40 estimate.
6. **Exercise the switches.** Pause with `AI_PAUSED` / `POST /admin/pause`, confirm the drawer shows "answers are paused for today"; confirm the per-visitor limit, a 409 after a deliberate catalogue mismatch, and the CSP report-only log shows no unexpected violations.
7. **Write the evidence and the gate checklist below** to `_roadmap/_ai-integration/_evidence/ax-20/launch-run.md`, and hand to the owner. Stop there.

## The owner's switch-on gate (all must be true; the owner records the yes)

1. AX-13's bar passed in the launch run: 100% of numbers grounded, 100% of refusals correct, right tool in at least 95% of runs, no run over 18,000 tokens, human read of 10 answers passed.
2. Freeze verified (`--verify` exit 0) and the HD5 branch recorded (A: transform-only, with held classes refused; B: ML cards in).
3. Worker: default `DAILY_NEURON_CAP` at most 10,000, per-visitor limit counted per question, pause switch tested, no key in the bundle (`scan-bundle` passed in CI on this build). This clears ship-blocker `AXW1` (check `board.py --ship` exits 0).
4. Turnstile works under the CSP (`HD11` applied); the owner decides separately whether to move the CSP from Report-Only to enforcing.
5. Capacity accepted: measured answers per day recorded and the owner accepts it (HD6).
6. The held-class refusals read correctly to the owner (traffic cost, ML, pit verdict, and AX-15a's dirty-air-dependent stubs, held because HD2 was ruled C), and no archived DA-03 number or chance-line number appears anywhere in the run.
7. Published data is the frozen snapshot, published to the CDN with its own owner yes.
8. The owner writes "switch on" in the log. Then the owner (not an agent) rebuilds with `VITE_ASSISTANT_ENABLED=true` and deploys (`make app-deploy` or the staged publish targets).
