## Why this item exists

Cards and rules let the model quote numbers. Nothing yet tells it which cards answer which question, what conclusion they allow, or which chains are forbidden. Today that knowledge exists only in an audit file and in the eval. No other item writes the system prompt: AX-08 ships a placeholder template, and AX-10 has no step for it.

## Decided

- In v1, only class T is live (AX-07). A recipe link that is T\*, WC, ML or the pit verdict is a **held link**. The recipe still lists it, and the answer gives the stub's held reason, never a number.
- Conclusions are worded as "consistent with" or "in line with". Never "because" or "caused by" from a count, a `computed_not_card` result, or context cards. In v1 no live card is a time-loss breakdown, so the model makes no causal split of lap time.
- Card kind names below follow the plan's v1 set. **AX-07's registry wins:** if a name differs there, update this file, not the registry.

**2. Chain recipes**

Ordered cards and calls for each type, the conclusion each allows, and the held link with its reason. Write them into `playbook.json` (step 6).

| type | Recipe (in order) | Allowed conclusion | Held link: reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| teammate_race | `race.coverage` → `race.teammate_gap` (both drivers) → `race.result` → `race.cautions` → `driver_race.pit_stops` | Gap with its paired-lap n and CI. `thin`: hedge. `suppressed`: "no reliable gap", no value. Coverage reason when unrated. Context "consistent with", never a breakdown | `race_lost.component`: "the time-loss breakdown includes the unvalidated flat dirty-air term; held until wake calibration" |
| season_standing | `season.teammate_gap` (rating, SE, CI, rank, `rank_separated_from_next`, n_races) | A rank only where separated from the next driver; otherwise "in a group whose intervals overlap" | era rating or ghost standings if T\*: stub reason |
| why_slow | `race.coverage` → `data_quality.race` → `race.cautions` → `driver_race.pit_stops` → `race.teammate_gap` | Lists the observed context and says the data cannot split the time loss into causes in v1 | `race_lost.component`, `lap_waterfall.component`, sector components |

**3. Forbidden chains, as model wording**

These come from audit-app.md section 3 and go into `system.md` in compressed form. Each names the rule code that enforces it.

- "Do not give one circuit's safety-car probability; the data shows no difference between circuits." `POOLED_SC_RATE_ONLY` (new, step 5)
- "Do not rank drivers across different skill measures; name the one you quote." `ONE_SKILL_MEASURE` (AX-09)
- "Do not count affinity and era ratings, ghost standings and era ratings, or Party Mode and Structural Pace as separate confirmations. They share inputs." `NOT_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE` (new)
- "Team Averages' car term and Structural Pace are two different car-pace numbers, not one." `NOT_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE`
- "Never say a driver would have won; Championship and Hidden Performance are reconstructions." `GHOST_IS_COUNTERFACTUAL` (AX-09)
- "Blind Test and Model Metrics are not out-of-sample." `IN_SAMPLE_FIT`, `CV_FOLD_NOT_CLEAN_HOLDOUT`
- "A grey lap-map cell is not free air; dirty-air cost is modelled, not measured." `LAP_MAP_GREY_CELL` (AX-09), `DIRTY_AIR_MODELLED_NOT_MEASURED` (TX-04)
- "No sector attribution and no cross-era pure-pace comparison; nothing about 2026." Sector cards are held (AX-15a); `PURE_PACE_WITHIN_ERA`; `NO_2026_DATA`
- "Quote only card or query numbers, never a number read from a page." Grounding check (AX-10)
- "A count does not explain a gap." `COUNT_NOT_CAUSE` (new)
- "Suppressed gap: no value. Thin gap: hedge." `RATING_TRUST_LABEL` (new)
- "A season rank only when the rank is separated from the next driver's." `RANK_WITHIN_CI` (new)

**4. Glossary of the six "skill" measures**

One model-facing line each, keyed by `policy_fields.skill_measure` (AX-09). The v1 status must equal the registry class.

1. `lap_residual`: `driver_skill_residual_s`, the lap time left after fuel, compound, car and dirty air. It means "unexplained", not "driver error". Held (T\*).
2. `consistency_residual`: the mean and spread of that residual (`driver_residual_mean_s`). Held (T\*).
3. `wet_proxy`: `driver_skill_proxy_mean_s`, the wet-race proxy. Held (T\*).
4. `teammate_paired_median`: `rating_s`, the median lap-time gap to the teammate on paired laps. T with `AIR_STATE_FILTER` if AX-07a confirms it.
5. `era_rating`: the season rating built on 4, bridged across teammates. Class per AX-07a.
6. `proxy_family`: `corner_skill_index`, the synthetic-teammate gap and qualifying-decomposed skill. These are three proxies, each on its own scale; class per AX-07a / AX-15a.
- Caveat line: `AIR_STATE_FILTER` means "laps were chosen with the air-state filter (traffic laps left out); the number is not a dirty-air estimate".

**5. Conclusion rules in `policy.json`** (24 to 29 rules; each with `fail`, `pass` and `not_fired` examples, written through `checkRule`)

- `RATING_TRUST_LABEL`. Trigger: `rating_trust_label` is `suppressed` or `thin` (a `policy_fields` key; do not reuse the isolation `trust_label`). Cite TX-01's `schema.yml` text.
- `RANK_WITHIN_CI`. Trigger: `rank_separated_from_next eq false`. Forbidden: an ordinal claim. Cite TX-06's `schema.yml` text.
- `POOLED_SC_RATE_ONLY`. Trigger: `source_page matches race-control`. Forbidden: a per-circuit probability.
- `NOT_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE`. Extend AX-09's `cards_distinct_values` trigger with an optional `where: {field, eq}` filter. Trigger: `{field: kind, min: 2, where: {field: shared_input_group, eq: <group>}}`, one trigger per group (`race_rating`, `constructor_pace`). Existing triggers do not change.
- `COUNT_NOT_CAUSE`. Trigger: `label eq computed_not_card` or `n_unit eq rows`. Forbidden: narrow causal patterns ("because of", "caused by", "due to").

Cite `sources` lines that `sources.node.test.ts` can verify. Keep the patterns narrow, so every passing example passes the whole policy.

**6. `playbook.json` and the `bundle` argument**

- `app/src/assistant/prompt/playbook.json`: `{version, types:[{type, examples[], recipe:[{kind|tool, scope_from, required}], allowed_conclusion, forbidden_codes[], held_links:[{kind, held_reason}]}]}`.
- `bundles.ts`: `resolveBundle(type, scope) → card ids`, at most 10, using AX-01's id pattern, with held stubs included.
- Add `bundle?: <enum of types with a card recipe>` to `get_fact_cards` in `tools.template.json`. AX-08's generator fills the enum from `playbook.json`, and AX-10 wires it into the dispatcher. One call then replaces up to five rounds. That is the main saving against the 12-40 answers a day (HD6).

**7. System prompt**

Write `app/src/assistant/prompt/system.md`, at most 1,200 tokens by `estimateTokens`; AX-08's generator fails above that. Order: (1) scope: 2018-2025, label v16, v1 transform-only; (2) grounding: every number comes from a card or a `result_id`, and the answer cites the ids; (3) label wording: measured, modelled, `computed_not_card`, unavailable, held; (4) "classify the question, then call `get_fact_cards({bundle})` first"; (5) the step 3 sentences; (6) the glossary; (7) refusals: the held reason, no estimate; (8) what-if questions get a link only; (9) style: plain words, define a term on first use, say what the number does not show.

The prompt carries no data values.

## Acceptance

- **Every recipe's cards exist or are held with a reason.** A test reads `playbook.json`, AX-07's registry and AX-15a's stubs. It fails on any kind that is neither live nor a stub, on a held link with no `held_reason`, and on a glossary status that disagrees with the registry class.
- **A planted forbidden-chain answer fails the checker.** One fixture per step 3 line (12), each with its accompanying cards. `checkAnswer` reports the named code, and the corrected answer passes the whole policy.
- On the plan's five demo cases, `resolveBundle('teammate_race', …)` returns at most 10 cards whose projection fits one 1,200-token tool slot: 2024_1 VER `ok`, 2020_14 HAM `suppressed`, 2018_1 RIC `NO_TELEMETRY`, 2021_16 `ALL_WET_NO_DRY_PAIRS`, and one SC-affected race.
- `system.md` is at most 1,200 tokens, and `gen-tools` builds with it and with the bundle enum.
- The 24 rules present at start are byte-for-byte unchanged, and `npx vitest run src/assistant` passes.
