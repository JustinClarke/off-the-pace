# ML-01 — Retrain models on 2018-2024 and score 2025 as the holdout; re-sign the model card

**Group:** 5-held-classes · **Depends on:** AX-20 (launch run), TX-29 (holdout moved to 2025, fits stop at 2024) · **Model / cost:** `opus-5`, 2-3d · **Stage:** SPEC (nothing run)

## Purpose
HD17 (2026-11-16) moved the holdout to 2025 and fixed the training window at 2018-2024. TX-29 moves the config and the transform fits and adds the guard test; it does not retrain the ML models. This item does: retrain the shipped ML models on 2018-2024, score 2025 once as the holdout, and re-sign the model card. It is after the AI work (owner priority, HD23 and the 2026-11-18 order: transform close, then AI work, then dirty air, ML retrain and UNC-01), so the assistant launches on the frozen snapshot first. AX-17 (ML cards) and AX-18 (pit verdict) depend on this item, so they also fall after launch.

## Method
1. Confirm TX-29 landed: `holdout_season` 2025, `HOLDOUT_SEASON = 2025`, every fit window ends at 2024, guard test green. Refuse to start otherwise.
2. Reconcile the naming clash noted on AX-17 (the plan calls the retrain v16; D11 ruled v16 = transform label, v17 = ML model) before any version is written.
3. Retrain every shipped model on 2018-2024 only, in a scratch folder, one thread; model selection and any threshold choice on folds inside 2018-2024.
4. Score 2025 exactly once. The score is recorded in the card with the date; it is never used to select, tune or gate a choice.
5. Recompute the conformal coverage on a calibration fold that was not used for selection (not 2025, not the selection fold), and report empirical coverage of the nominal interval on 2025 as a separate, once-only line.
6. Re-sign the model card (version, data window, holdout, coverage, in-envelope definition, checksum) and update `ml/models/manifest.json`. Owner approves the signing.
7. Publishing the retrained models to the app and the assistant is a separate owner step with a new freeze; it is not part of this item.

## Acceptance test
- 2025 is scored once; a guard shows no training, selection or calibration row has `race_year >= 2025`, and the score file records a single evaluation.
- Conformal coverage is recomputed on a fold not used for selection; the nominal-versus-empirical gap is reported per model; a model whose gap exceeds the card's stated tolerance is marked unvalidated, not shipped.
- The card states window 2018-2024, holdout 2025, and does not claim the 2025 result was blind if any earlier step (the 02c admission gate, per the TX-29 doc) used 2025 as a selection fold: that history is stated in the card.
- No 2026 data is read (HD21).

## Risks
- 2025 was already consumed as a selection fold by an earlier admission gate (TX-29 doc, `ml/src/card.py` HOLDOUT_NOTE), so it is a holdout in policy but not a virgin one; the card must say so, and no claim of an untouched test is made.
- Retrained models change every ML card and the cliff probabilities; the assistant snapshot must be re-frozen (AX-19) after publishing, or the frozen launch breaks.
- Version-naming clash (above) can write the wrong label into published files.
- The retrain must not be scored against the label that DA/ghost items later change (AX-15b: if the flat 0.331 tax moves, the label moves and the models need another pass).
