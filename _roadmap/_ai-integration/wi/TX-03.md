**Group:** 1 transform · **Depends on:** `TX-02` (same marts; runs third in lane M: TX-01 → TX-02 → TX-03 → TX-06) · **Blocker:** none for the aggregate-level fix; any change at lap grain or to a label goes to the owner first. · **Model / cost:** opus-5, 0.5 d (build log). · **Priority:** P0.

**Sources (checked to exist):** `transform/models/intermediate/int_lap_residual_decomposed.sql` (fill at lines 284-296, header lines 50-58), `transform/models/intermediate/int_dirty_air_tax_component.sql`, plus the three aggregates the fix lands in: `transform/models/marts/mart_race_driver_summary.sql` (line 28), `mart_driver_season_summary.sql` (`components` CTE), `mart_metric_benchmark.sql` (lines 35-39), and `transform/models/marts/schema.yml`. ## The problem (re-verified 2026-11-07 on the audit's scratch build)

`int_lap_residual_decomposed.sql:285-296` writes `dirty_air_tax_s`, `dirty_air_tax_se_s` and `cumulative_dirty_air_tax_race_s` as `0.0` wherever `int_dirty_air_tax_component` has no row. The comment says the fill "never reaches driver_skill_residual_s". True, but it reaches every mean.

- `fct_lap_residuals` has **11,588** laps with no dirty-air row. They are exactly the laps with `pace_delta_s IS NULL` (11,588 both ways; no lap with NULL `pace_delta_s` has a row). **6,460** of them are `ml_eligible` (**audit reproduces**): all 622 of 2018_1 and 732 of 2018_2, then 2022_11 (189), 2025_23 (120), 2023_10 (118), and others.
- A measured 0.0 is common and real: 103,280 `ml_eligible` laps have `dirty_air_tax_s = 0` (clean air). So 0 alone cannot tell measured from unmeasured.
- `mart_race_driver_summary.mean_dirty_air_tax_s = 0.0` on all 16 drivers of 2018_1 and all 18 of 2018_2 (**reproduces**).
- Measured-only means, on the scratch build: 40 race-mart rows become NULL (34 in 2018_1/2, 6 elsewhere, e.g. 2021_6 OCO, 2024_3 VER); 2,331 of 3,086 non-NULL rows change, max |Δ| 0.248 s, 405 by more than 0.01 s. Season mart: 172 of 173 rows change, max |Δ| 0.013 s. Benchmark dirty_air rows, mean old → new: 2018 0.0782 → 0.0865 (n 15,845 → 14,333), 2025 0.1134 → 0.1186; p50 stays 0.0 every season.

## Method (default: aggregate level only; no lap value moves)

1. In each of the three aggregates, average dirty air **over measured laps only**: a lap is measured when it has a row in `int_dirty_air_tax_component` **and** its tax is not NULL. Use an explicit `LEFT JOIN {{ ref('int_dirty_air_tax_component') }}` on `lap_id` (marts already read intermediates), not the `pace_delta_s` proxy, so the rule does not depend on a coincidence. In `mart_metric_benchmark` filter the dirty_air arm only; the other five metrics keep their rows.
2. Add `n_dirty_air_measured_laps` to `mart_race_driver_summary` and `mart_driver_season_summary`. `mean_dirty_air_tax_s` is NULL when it is 0. Add `dirty_air_unavailable_reason` (`NO_MEASURED_LAPS`) as its own column. Do not reuse TX-02's `residual_unavailable_reason`: its "reason iff missing" test is on `n_clean_laps`, and a race can have clean laps with no measured dirty air.
3. `schema.yml`: `mean_dirty_air_tax_s` = "mean over laps with a measured air state; NULL when none"; the benchmark description says the dirty_air row excludes unmeasured laps. Fix the `int_lap_residual_decomposed` header and inline comment (lines 50-58 and 280-284) to say the 0.0 does reach lap-grain consumers.
4. **Do not change** the `CASE WHEN da.lap_id IS NULL THEN 0.0` fill itself, or add a column to `fct_lap_residuals`, in this item. Both would move a `fct_*` hash (the byte oracle fails on that), `transform/tests/data_profile.baseline.json`, and app pages that read the column (`app/src/features/dirty-air-cost/`, `lap-waterfall/`, `constructor-team-averages/`, `race-lost/`). List those consumers in the hand-back as a follow-up for the owner. **No lap-grain flag is added**, so AP-01 tells unmeasured dirty air apart by `pace_delta_s IS NULL`. The test `assert_dirty_air_fill_only_where_base_unmeasured` below turns that into a tested invariant, and that test is what AP-01's dependency on this item is for.

**Must not move:** label v16 and `label_stability_baseline.csv` (`assert_prior_season_labels_frozen` must still pass); any `fct_*` or `int_*` table; any column of the three marts other than the dirty-air mean and the new columns; the benchmark's other 40 rows.

## Acceptance

- 2018_1 and 2018_2: `mean_dirty_air_tax_s` NULL with a reason on all 34 rows; `n_dirty_air_measured_laps = 0`.
- No race or season row has `mean_dirty_air_tax_s` computed over a lap with no dirty-air row (test below).
- The change counts above re-measured and reported (40 / 2,331 / 172, benchmark means), or the difference explained.
- Oracle against `$S/before.json`: every `fct_*` OK; non-fct drift only in the three marts.

## Tests to add

- `assert_dirty_air_mean_excludes_unmeasured.sql`: recompute each mart's mean from `fct_lap_residuals` ⋈ `int_dirty_air_tax_component` over measured laps; fail on any |Δ| > 1e-9 or on a non-NULL mean with zero measured laps.
- `assert_dirty_air_fill_only_where_base_unmeasured.sql`: in `fct_lap_residuals`, no dirty-air row ⇔ `pace_delta_s IS NULL`. It guards the header's claim and fails if a future change lets the 0.0 reach the residual.
- `schema.yml`: `accepted_range min 0` on `n_dirty_air_measured_laps`. Neither test may require a specific race id (CI fixture: 2020 Monza, 2023 Bahrain, 2024 São Paulo).
