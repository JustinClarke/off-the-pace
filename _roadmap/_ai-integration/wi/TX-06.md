## The problem (re-verified 2026-11-07 on the audit's scratch build, 173 rows)

`mart_driver_season_summary` keeps `ci_low`/`ci_high` and `n_races` from `int_era_normalized_driver_rating`. It drops these:

| Upstream column | What it is on the scratch build |
|---|---|
| `era_adjusted_rating_se_s` | the SE; the CI is exactly `rating ± 1.96 × SE` (max error 8e-17) |
| `rating_confidence` | 0.167 to 0.828 (**audit's min 0.167 reproduces**) |
| `total_clean_laps_n` | laps behind the season rating |
| `bridge_driver_anchor_flag` | TRUE on 130 of 173 |
| `low_anchor_sample_flag` | FALSE on all 173. Carrying it adds nothing today; carry it anyway so a future TRUE is visible |

Every upstream row reaches the mart (0 dropped by the primary-team join).

**Rank and percentile** (`teammate_gap_rank`, `teammate_gap_percentile`) are point orderings with no uncertainty. In 2024, ranks 2-5 (ALB −0.2521, ALO −0.2375, NOR −0.2186, HUL −0.2170) span **0.035 s**, inside CIs 0.406-0.451 s wide (**audit reproduces**). The CI of 171 of 173 drivers overlaps the CI of the driver ranked next to them. A CI-overlap rank range ("could rank anywhere from best to worst") is uninformative: median width 19 places, against fields of about 20 (VER 2024: 1-15; ALB: 1-19).

## Method

1. Carry the five columns through `ratings` and the final select under their upstream names, but rename `era_adjusted_rating_se_s` to `rating_se_s` to match `rating_s`. No value changes.
2. **Rank:** keep the values. Rewrite the `teammate_gap_rank` and `teammate_gap_percentile` descriptions (and the header comment, lines 9-11) to say: a point ordering of `rating_s`; adjacent ranks are almost never separated by the CI (171 of 173); quote only with the CI. Add `rank_separated_from_next` (BOOLEAN): this driver's `ci_high` < the next-ranked driver's `ci_low` in the same season. It is the one cheap, honest signal. Do not add a rank range (see above) unless the owner asks for one.
3. If TX-01's owner ruling adopted `rating_trust_label`, add the same column here with thresholds the owner sets for the season grain (for example on `n_races` or `rating_confidence`). Otherwise skip it and say so in the hand-back. Do not invent thresholds.
4. `schema.yml`: describe each new column; `not_null` on `rating_se_s`, `rating_confidence`, `total_clean_laps_n` and both flags; `accepted_range` 0-1 on `rating_confidence`.

**Must not move:** `rating_s`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`, `n_races`, `teammate_gap_rank`, `teammate_gap_percentile` and every other existing column; the row count (173); `int_era_normalized_driver_rating` and every other model; label v16 and `label_stability_baseline.csv`; every `fct_*` hash in your own `$S/before.json`.
