## The problem (re-verified 2026-11-07)

- `raw_dim_events.csv` holds 6 rows, all 2021 (E001-E006). `dim_events.sql:12` still says "This references your brand-new dim_events.csv file" (the audit said line 11).
- It is exported: `export_app_data.py:57` (TABLES), `:178` (CANARY_TABLES) and `:562` (`total_events` in the stats block). The served `app/public/data/_manifest.json` has `total_events: 6` and a `dim_events` table entry.
- **No reader.** No dbt model refs `dim_events`. In `app/src` the only hit is the type field `total_events: number` (`manifest.ts:20`); nothing renders it. No test reads it. `stg_events` (same seed) also has no consumer: `transform/models/intermediate/README.md:41` says `int_event_corrections` reads it, but that model reads `stg_laps` and `stg_pits`, not `stg_events`.
- **Content is wrong, checked against the warehouse** (scratch copy, `stg_results`, run from `transform/`):
  - E004 "Verstappen mechanical DNF" at 2021_14 (Monza): `stg_results` has VER status `Collision`, and HAM `Collision` too. Not mechanical.
  - E002 "Hamilton grid penalty" at 2021_10 (Silverstone): HAM grid 2, finish 1. Grid 2 is his sprint result, so there was no grid drop (the real penalty was a 10 s time penalty).
  - The other four rows were not checked against data and need not be: the table goes.
- Eight of its ten columns are undocumented in `schema.yml`.
