# TX-13 - export a calibration flag

**Group:** 1 transform · **Depends on:** none · **Model/cost:** sonnet-5, 0.5d.

**Sources:** `transform/seeds/circuit_reference.csv`, `transform/models/reference/dim_circuits.sql` (and its yml), `scripts/export_app_data.py`, `app/src/assistant/honesty/policy.json` (rule CALIBRATION_FLAGGED_CIRCUIT).

## Problem
`calibration_flag` exists in the circuit_reference seed but not in `dim_circuits` or the export, so CALIBRATION_FLAGGED_CIRCUIT has no producer and can never fire.

## Method
Add the column to `dim_circuits` (and its schema yml with a description of what the flag means: read the seed's comments), include it in the exported `dim_circuits`, add a test that the exported column equals the seed. Run dbt only on your own scratch copy named dev.duckdb at 1 thread, compare byte hashes against your own before-snapshot: no other model's hash may change. Never open data/dev.duckdb or data/ci.duckdb.

## Acceptance
`dim_circuits.calibration_flag` present, non-null where the seed has a value, equal to the seed on every row; every other model hash unchanged; export `--check` on a scratch export passes. Do not commit.
