ASSUMPTIONS AND BUILDER CHOICES - each is a builder choice for the owner to review
  1. REF_MIN_N = 2: a pass has a "measured" reference when at least 2 own clean passes are in the window.
     The spec does not give a minimum. Reference value = median of donor segment times.
  2. Corner class from the median apex speed of clear-air passes in that race (no car ahead within 3 s at
     the apex, lap not neutralised/restart/pit). Speed at the nearest sample to the circuit_info corner
     point. Segments and classes are built per race, not once per circuit layout across seasons.
  3. Segment start ("braking point") is the corner's median brake onset (last rising edge of the brake
     signal within 300 m before the apex) over the same clear-air passes, fixed per corner per race, so
     every pass of a corner is timed over the same distance window relative to its apex. The per-pass
     brake onset is stored (brake_offset_m) but not used to cut the segment.
  4. Flat kinks: a corner where fewer than 50% of clear-air passes have a brake onset is a flat kink;
     its passes carry reason "flat_kink" and get no segment (the spec merges kinks with the straight, and
     straights are not part of this table). 666 of 2,812 race-corners; 508,254 passes.
  5. Leader (car ahead) = the latest apex at the same corner by another car within 30 s before the
     follower's apex (on-track crossing order at the apex, not FastF1 DriverAhead). Car behind within 1 s
     = another car's apex within 1 s after the follower's apex.
  6. Gap = mean over 26 points of (follower time at d) - (leader time at d), d running 300 m to 50 m
     before the segment start, each car's distance measured from its own apex. Can be negative when the
     order changed inside the gap window (a pass); 10,504 kept passes have a negative gap; none is clean.
  7. Tags: "lapped" when the leader is on a lower lap number and the gap is under 3 s; "yielding" when a
     BLUE flag for the follower's number falls inside the follower's lap; "lapped+yielding" when both.
     Tags do not exclude. Yielding passes are never used as reference donors.
  8. Deleted laps (laps.Deleted) are NOT excluded, because the spec does not list them.
  9. Leader pit check uses the leader's lap number at its apex of that corner (pit in or out on that lap).
 10. Neutralised = the follower's lap [LapStartTime, Time] overlaps any track status 2 (yellow anywhere),
     4 (SC), 5 (red), 6 (VSC) or 7 (VSC ending). Track status 2 is a whole-track yellow signal, so any
     local yellow excludes the whole lap for everyone (broader than "yellow-sector laps").
 11. Restart = the end of a 4/5/6/7 period; the driver's first 2 laps STARTING at or after it are
     excluded (the lap running across the restart is already neutralised).
 12. Wet = compound INTERMEDIATE or WET, or weather rainfall_flag true at any sample inside the lap.
 13. Lap 1 and each driver's last lap never produce a pass: a lap needs the laps either side in the
     telemetry so the windows can cross the line. So reason lap_1 counts 0 by construction.
 14. OffTrack: exclusion fires when any telemetry Status != OnTrack in [segment start - 300 m,
     apex + 100 m] for the follower or leader. In bronze, OffTrack samples are almost all on lap 1 (the
     grid); 1,655 samples sit on later laps. After the higher-priority reasons the off_track count is 0.
     The check does NOT detect track-limits excursions.
 15. Compound None / nan / empty -> "UNKNOWN"; references match on the stored compound string, and a pass
     with no stint number gets no reference.
 16. circuit_info fallback: if the race's own corners match the telemetry for under 90% of (lap, corner)
     checks, or are missing, the same race slug's circuit_info from the season before or after is used;
     the source season and the match share are stored per pass (circuit_info_season, circuit_match).
