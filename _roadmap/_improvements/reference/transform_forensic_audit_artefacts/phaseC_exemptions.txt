## int_sc_hazard_history ['season'] accepted
    per_season_global totals every circuit within one season, the input to the season-lagged pooled EB prior. Pooling ACROSS CIRCUITS is the point of a pooled prior; pooling across seasons is what would leak, and this group cannot do it because season is the key. The lag is applied afterwards, in global_prior, which ends at 1 PRECEDING on the season axis -- so the prior a season-S row shrinks toward is itself built from seasons < S. Shrinking toward an all-time pooled rate is the back-door version of the leak this whole model was rebuilt to close, and this exemption is the record that it was considered.
