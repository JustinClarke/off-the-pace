A model that fits its training data proves nothing on its own. The blind-test scoreboard puts the shipped models' predictions next to what actually happened, lap by lap, for the season you pick. It becomes a true blind test once the designated holdout season, 2026, starts to ingest. Until then every season it can show was part of the training set, so read it as in-sample fit, not as out-of-sample proof. Every number is read from the model artefacts, not hand-typed.

## What it shows

Three panels score the models on the selected season:

- **Predicted-vs-actual scatter**: each point is one lap. The x-axis is the actual cumulative degradation jump over the next five laps (`next_5_lap_cumulative_jump_s`); the y-axis is the model's p50 prediction. Points on the 45° diagonal are perfect calls. The p10 / p90 band is the 80% conformal envelope.
- **Interval coverage rug**: empirical coverage is the fraction of laps where the actual jump falls inside [p10, p90]. The target is 80% (conformal calibration from the training fold). Green bars hit; red bars miss.
- **Cliff-class confusion matrix**: predicted laps-until-cliff class (rows) versus actual class (columns), row-normalised so each cell's intensity is the fraction of that predicted class landing in each actual bucket. The teal diagonal is correct; red off-diagonal is error.

<Note>
  All numbers are read from `mart_degradation_predictions.parquet`, joined to `fct_cliff_prediction_features` for the actuals and `fct_stint_features` for stint length. No values are hand-typed.
</Note>

## How to use it

1. **Read the scatter's spread around the diagonal** tight is well-calibrated; a tilt means systematic over- or under-prediction.
2. **Check the coverage rug against 80%** consistent green means the intervals are honest on this season.
3. **Read the confusion diagonal** a strong teal diagonal means the cliff classifier rarely confuses adjacent classes.
4. **Re-check after a season ingests** once 2026 races arrive, they are scored by a model that never saw them.

## Reading the signal correctly

The shipped `v14` models were refit on every ingested season (2018–2025), so the predictions here are in-sample: they show how the models behave on laps they were trained on, which flatters both the scatter and the coverage. The honest out-of-sample numbers are the final cross-validation fold, 2025, scored by a model trained only on 2018–2024; those are on [Model metrics](/app/model-metrics). The conformal band targets 80% coverage by design, so slight over-coverage is expected even out of sample.

<Warning>
  Nothing on this page is out-of-sample until 2026 data ingests. Even the 2025 cross-validation fold is a validation fold rather than a clean holdout, because it was also used to decide which features to admit.
</Warning>

## Data source

The chart reads `mart_degradation_predictions` (the shipped models' scored laps), joined to `fct_cliff_prediction_features` for actuals and `fct_stint_features` for stint length, via DuckDB-Wasm in the browser.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `predicted_degradation_jump_p10_s` / `predicted_degradation_jump_s` / `predicted_degradation_jump_p90_s` | Quantile predictions; p50 is the scatter y-axis, p10–p90 the band |
| `actual_degradation_jump_s` | Observed cumulative jump over the next five laps (the scatter x-axis), from `next_5_lap_cumulative_jump_s` |
| `predicted_cliff_class` / `actual_cliff_class` | Confusion-matrix rows and columns |
| `predicted_remaining_stint_life_laps` / `actual_remaining_stint_life_laps` | Stint-life prediction and the realised remainder (`stint_length_laps - lap_in_stint`) |
| `is_in_envelope` | Whether the lap is training-eligible (the same filter the models were fit on) |

`mart_degradation_predictions` also carries `is_holdout`, which marks laps in the designated holdout season (2026). It is FALSE on every row until 2026 ingests.

For the full evaluation methodology, see the [ML validation page](/ml/validation).
