Air state classification and thermal load from dirty air. One row per stint-lap. Classifies air state per sector (free_air, tow_zone, drs_train, dirty_air) and accumulates cumulative thermal load on tyre surface and bulk using exponential moving average. S2 (the dirty-air third) is classified on the median gap alone: &lt; 1.5 s is dirty_air whatever DRS did (F48, WI-15a). drs_train and tow_zone arise only on the straight thirds (S1/S3), where DRS still splits a sub-1 s follow. FD6 (CO-04): a lap with no measured gap to the car ahead is 'free_air' / 0.0 only for the race leader (end-of-lap position 1), who has no car ahead. For any other car, or an unknown position, every air-state term (air_state_dominant, dirty_air_share_lap, tow_benefit_lap_s, dirty_air_intensity, time_in_dirty_air_s and the thermal loads) is NULL: unmeasured, not clean air. The NULL wins over the SC/VSC zeroing. A thermal load is also NULL while an unmeasured lap of the same stint is inside its lookback. 2018_1 Australia and 2018_2 Bahrain have no telemetry in bronze, so nearly all of their non-leader laps are NULL here; downstream that excludes both races from the field base (int_field_pace_curve) and loses their residuals and training labels. Elsewhere this is 2-32 laps a season. The dominant state's tie-break is declared (T7): most frequent measured sector state, ties to dirty_air &gt; drs_train &gt; tow_zone &gt; free_air.
